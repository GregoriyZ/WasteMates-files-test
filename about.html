Our story

Built on doing it right.

WasteMates is a Mooroolbark-based rubbish removal company serving Melbourne's outer east, providing upfront-priced junk removal, tenant clean outs, yard clean ups, light demolition, deceased estate clearing and hard & bulky waste collection for households and businesses, 7 days a week.

WasteMates started with a simple frustration: rubbish removal that turned up late, quoted vague and left a mess behind. We reckoned Melbourne's outer east deserved better — a crew that shows up on time, gives an honest price, and treats your place like their own.

Today we clear everything from a single mattress to whole-house and commercial jobs — based in Mooroolbark and working right across the Yarra Ranges, Maroondah and Knox suburbs, plus greater Melbourne for bigger jobs. We hand-load it all, sort what can be recycled, and sweep up before we go.

We turn up in a tipper truck with everything we need — no waiting on a skip bin, no separate trailer booking. One call, one crew, most jobs done in a single visit.

No call centres, no runaround — just a friendly local team, ready when you are.
