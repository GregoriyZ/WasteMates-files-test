Quick answers

Rubbish removal, sorted.

How much does it cost?

WasteMates' rubbish removal starts from $200 per job. Every quote is confirmed upfront on site, with no hidden fees.

Same-day removal?

In most cases, yes — WasteMates can often fit in same-day rubbish removal, especially for calls made early in the day.

Which areas?

WasteMates is based in Mooroolbark and services the surrounding Yarra Ranges, Maroondah and Knox suburbs — plus greater Melbourne for bigger jobs.

What can't you take?

WasteMates can't legally remove asbestos, hazardous chemicals or medical/biohazard waste. Almost everything else is fine.
