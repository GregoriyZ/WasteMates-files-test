How our pricing works

1. Rubbish by the load

Loose rubbish is priced by the cubic metre, which is a space 1 metre wide, 1 metre long and 1 metre high. The rate depends on what it is: green waste is $75, general waste is $100 and heavy waste is $150 per cubic metre.

2. Big items by the item

Mattresses, couches, fridges, white goods and tyres each have a set price, starting from $40 per item. These are charged per item, separately from your cubic metres.

3. Add it up

Most jobs are a mix of both. We add your load and your items together and confirm one fixed price on site before we lift a thing.
